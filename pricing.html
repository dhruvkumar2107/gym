Membership FAQ

Got questions? We have answers.

How do I sign up for a membership?
Choose your preferred plan above, click "Join Now", complete the online checkout via Razorpay, and your membership is activated instantly. You can also visit our gym at Andheri West, Mumbai to sign up in person.
Is there a joining fee or hidden charges?
Absolutely not. We charge zero joining fee and there are no hidden charges. The price you see on the plan is the price you pay. No surprise deductions ever.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets through our secure Razorpay payment gateway. All transactions are 100% safe and encrypted.
Can I pay in installments?
Monthly plans are billed each month. For quarterly, half-yearly, and annual plans, the full amount is collected at the time of purchase. We do offer flexible EMI options on select plans through Razorpay.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, memberships of 3 months or longer are eligible for a freeze. You can freeze for up to 14 days per quarter for medical reasons or travel. Contact our front desk or reach out via WhatsApp to request a freeze.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
You may cancel within 3 days of purchase for a full refund if no sessions have been used. After that, memberships are non-refundable but you can transfer or gift your remaining duration to another person with prior approval.
Do plans auto-renew?
No, your membership does not auto-renew. You will receive a reminder before your plan expires, and you can choose to renew with the same plan or upgrade to a different one.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes! You can upgrade to a higher-tier plan at any time. The remaining value of your current plan will be adjusted toward the new plan. Simply contact us or visit the front desk to process the upgrade.
